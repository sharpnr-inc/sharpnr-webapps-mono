Sharp-Marketplace works because every participant is known. It is a trusted, verified network — not an open listing site.

## Organizations only

Transactions on Sharp-Marketplace are organization-to-organization (O2O) only. Every buyer and seller is an organization on the Sharp platform — there are no anonymous accounts.

## Secure business identity verification

Every organization's business identity is verified securely, so suppliers and service providers you find on the marketplace are who they claim to be.

## One-click onboarding

Joining the marketplace takes one click — organization verification is automatic, because the platform already knows your organization from [signup and onboarding](/signup-and-onboarding). There is no separate marketplace registration process.

## Verified suppliers and providers

Buyers see verified suppliers and service providers, which is what makes [AI-powered discovery](/sharp-marketplace/buying-and-selling) and RFQ comparison meaningful — every recommendation is a real, verified business.

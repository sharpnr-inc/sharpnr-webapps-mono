Welcome to Sharpnr, your go-to resource for all the guides and documentation you need to get started with sharpnr.

## What is Sharpnr?

Sharpnr is a platform for the business and organization for Business Operations, providing tools and resources to help you manage and optimize your operations effectively.
Sharpnr offers a wide range of cloud based services and solutions designed to streamline your business processes, enhance productivity, and drive growth.

## Architecture Overview

Sharpnr's architecture is built on a robust and scalable cloud infrastructure, ensuring high availability and performance. The platform is designed to be flexible and adaptable, allowing businesses of all sizes to leverage its capabilities effectively. Sharpnr integrates various services and tools to provide a comprehensive solution for business operations management.

## The apps

Sharpnr is one platform made of many applications. They share a single sign-in,
one organization structure, and one set of financial records — so work started in
one app finishes in another without reconciliation.

### Platform services

| App | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Orchestrator](/orchestrator) | Command centre — provision and configure every other app |
| [Cortex](/cortex) | IT controls, access management, audit logs |
| [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting) | Finance, invoicing, double-entry accounting |
| [Sharp-HR](/sharp-hrms) | HRMS, payroll, attendance, leave |
| [Sharp-IAM](/sharp-iam) | Employee portal — tasks, payslips, requests |
| [Sharp-CRM](/sharp-marketing) | CRM, leads, campaigns |
| [Cables](/cables) | Messaging, channels, calls |
| [Silo](/silo) | Storage, data lake, data warehouse |
| [Folio](/folio) | Documents, sheets, whiteboards |
| [Sharp-Marketplace](/sharp-marketplace) | B2B and O2O trading |
| Stockwave | Inventory and stock management |

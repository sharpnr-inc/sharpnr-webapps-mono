SharpIAM gives every employee a single, secure place for their working life.

## Your dashboard

After a secure employee login, the dashboard brings together what matters today — tasks, notifications, announcements, and quick access to everything below.

## Time off and pay

- **Leave applications & approval tracking** — apply for leave and follow its approval status without chasing anyone.
- **Payroll & payslip access** — view and download payslips.
- **Attendance & timesheets** — see your recorded attendance and time.
- **Holiday calendar** — know the organization's holidays in advance.

## Work and tasks

- **View & manage assigned tasks** — what's on your plate, tracked in one place.
- **Shift & schedule viewing** — see your upcoming shifts and rosters.
- **Performance reviews & goals** — follow your reviews and objectives through the cycle.
- **Activity logs & work history** — your record of what you've done.

## Requests and resources

- **Expense & reimbursement requests** — submit claims and track them to payout.
- **Document access & downloads** — contracts, policies, and the documents you need.
- **Profile & personal information management** — keep your own details current.

## Staying connected

- **Company announcements** — organization news reaches you in the portal.
- **Organization directory** — find colleagues across companies and branches.
- **Notifications & reminders** — approvals, deadlines, and updates find you.

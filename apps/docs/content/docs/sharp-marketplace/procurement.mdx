A marketplace deal isn't done until it's in your systems. Sharp-Marketplace connects trading directly to the Sharp apps that run your business.

## Purchase orders & invoices from transactions

Create purchase orders and invoices directly from marketplace transactions — no re-keying the deal into your accounting system. Transactions connect with [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting) and your Business Operation Apps, so a purchase agreed on the marketplace flows through to books and inventory.

## Procurement & supplier management

Manage suppliers and procurement as an ongoing relationship, not one-off transactions — who you buy from, on what terms, and how they perform.

## A unified procurement workflow

Across the ecosystem, the pieces work as one flow:

1. **Discover** a supplier on the marketplace ([Buying & Selling](/sharp-marketplace/buying-and-selling)).
2. **Negotiate** — RFQs out, quotations back via [Cables](/cables) or your preferred channel.
3. **Order** — purchase orders created from the transaction.
4. **Account** — invoices and [purchase-to-inventory accounting](/sharp-accounting/platform-integration) land in Sharp-Accounting automatically.

## Inter-organization workflows

Beyond individual purchases, organizations manage ongoing inter-org workflows and share accounting and billing data securely with trading partners — the platform handles the boundary between organizations.

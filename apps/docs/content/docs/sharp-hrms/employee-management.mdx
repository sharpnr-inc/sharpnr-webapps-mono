Sharp-HR manages the full employee lifecycle — from the job posting that finds someone to the offboarding that closes their file.

## Employee information management

Every employee's record in one place: personal information, employment details, history, and documents. Records span companies and branches, so a multi-company organization manages its whole workforce from one system.

## Recruitment & hiring

Run recruitment through Sharp-HR — openings, candidates, and hiring decisions — so new hires flow straight into the employee system they'll be managed in.

## Onboarding & offboarding

Structured onboarding gets new employees set up — records, access, assignments — and offboarding closes everything down cleanly when someone leaves, including access managed through the platform's [session controls](/orchestrator/access-and-security).

## Organization chart & directory

The org chart and employee directory keep structure and people findable — who reports to whom, who works where, and how to reach them. The directory is also available to employees in [SharpIAM](/sharp-iam).

## Documents & compliance management

Employment contracts, certifications, policies, and the compliance documents HR is accountable for — stored, tracked, and connected to the employee records they belong to.

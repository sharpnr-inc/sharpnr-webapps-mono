Managers use the same portal as their teams — with the added tools to lead them.

## Team management

See and manage your team in one view: who's in, who's on leave, what's assigned, and how work is progressing.

## Manager approvals

Requests from your team arrive for your decision — leave applications, expense claims, and other approvals — actionable directly in the portal, with employees seeing the outcome instantly on their side.

## Reports & analytics

Reports and analytics give managers visibility into their team's attendance, tasks, and performance — the operational picture behind day-to-day decisions. Organization-wide HR analytics live in [Sharp-HR](/sharp-hrms).

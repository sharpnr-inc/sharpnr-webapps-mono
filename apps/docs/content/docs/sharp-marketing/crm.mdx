The CRM core of Sharp-Marketing gives sales teams one system for everyone they talk to and every deal in flight.

## Leads, contacts, and accounts

- **Lead management** — capture and qualify prospective customers, from first touch onward.
- **Contact management** — the people you deal with, with full history.
- **Account management** — the organizations behind the contacts.

Leads arrive from many sources — [forms with lead capture and scoring](/sharp-marketing/campaigns-and-automation), campaigns, and customer acquisition efforts — and land in one pipeline.

## Opportunities, pipeline, and deals

Track opportunities through your sales funnel with pipeline management and deal tracking — what's in play, at what stage, at what value, and what needs to happen next.

## Sales activity

Day-to-day selling is managed in the same place:

- **Activity timeline** — every interaction with a contact or account, in order.
- **Task & follow-up management** — nothing slips because nobody owned the follow-up.
- **Meeting & call scheduling** — book and track meetings and calls with prospects and customers.

## Documents & proposals

Manage documents and proposals alongside the deals they belong to, with content authored in [Folio](/folio). Quotations can be sent directly to [Cables](/cables) or the recipient's preferred communication channel — including to other organizations in [Sharp-Marketplace](/sharp-marketplace) deals.

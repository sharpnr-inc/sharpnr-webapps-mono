Cortex brings enterprise-grade security governance to the Sharp platform — every access is tracked, policies are defined centrally, and administration scales across organizations.

## Audit logs & activity tracking

Every access event and administrative action flows into audit logs: who accessed which resource, what permissions changed, which credentials were issued or revoked, and when. This gives organizations:

- **Accountability** — a complete record of activity across the platform's resources.
- **Compliance evidence** — an audit trail for security and regulatory requirements, complementing the financial audit trails in [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting).
- **Incident response** — the history needed to investigate and respond to security concerns.

## Security policies & compliance

Define security policies centrally and apply them across the organization. Policies express your organization's security posture — who may hold which roles, how credentials are handled, what access requires review — in one place rather than per app.

Policy enforcement with approval workflows (requiring sign-off before sensitive access is granted) is on the [roadmap](/cortex/roadmap).

## Multi-organization administration

For groups that operate multiple organizations on the Sharp platform, Cortex supports multi-organization administration — IT teams manage access, credentials, and policies across all of them from a single control plane.

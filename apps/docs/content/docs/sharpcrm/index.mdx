## What is Sharp-CRM?

Sharp-CRM is the customer relationship management (CRM) and marketing automation application of the Sharp platform, covering the entire customer lifecycle — acquisition, campaigns, sales pipeline, engagement, and loyalty.

**App:** [sharpcrm.sharpnr.com](https://sharpcrm.sharpnr.com)

## What can you do with Sharp-CRM?

| Capability                | Description                                                              |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CRM                       | Leads, contacts, accounts, opportunities, pipeline, and deal tracking      |
| Campaigns & Automation    | Email, SMS, WhatsApp, and social campaigns with journey automation         |
| Customer Engagement       | Loyalty and rewards, referrals, feedback, surveys, and the customer portal |
| Analytics                 | Conversion tracking, ROI, attribution, dashboards, and KPIs                |
| Integrations              | Third-party integrations and deep connections across the Sharp platform    |

Beyond core HR administration, Sharp-HR handles the day-to-day interactions between the organization and its people.

## Employee self-service

Employees handle their own HR needs — leave, payslips, timesheets, profile updates, document access — through [SharpIAM](/sharp-iam), the employee portal, instead of queueing requests through the HR team.

## Expense claims & reimbursements

Employees submit expense claims, managers approve them, and reimbursements flow through to [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting/financial-management) — one workflow from receipt to books.

## Complaints & grievance management

A structured, confidential channel for complaints and grievances, tracked from submission to resolution.

## Asset assignment & tracking

Track which company assets — laptops, phones, equipment — are assigned to which employees, and reclaim them cleanly at offboarding.

## Announcements & internal communication

Publish announcements to the whole organization or specific companies and branches. Announcements reach employees in SharpIAM, and richer team communication runs through [Cables](/cables).

## Benefits & compensation management

Manage benefits and compensation structures across the organization, connected to payroll.

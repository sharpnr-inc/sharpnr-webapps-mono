## What is Cables?

Cables is the secure messaging, voice call, and video conferencing application of the Sharp platform — the unified communication layer connecting every service in the Sharp ecosystem. Conversations in Cables aren't isolated from your work: records, documents, invoices, tickets, and tasks from other Sharp apps can be shared and discussed directly inside them.

Communication is fast, encrypted, and low-latency, with enterprise-grade security and role-based access control throughout.

**App:** [cables.sharpnr.com](https://cables.sharpnr.com)

## What can you do with Cables?

| Capability             | Description                                                                  |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Messaging              | One-to-one chats, group chats, organization-wide channels, and threads        |
| Calls & Meetings       | Voice calls, video conferencing, and screen sharing                           |
| Context Integration    | Share records, invoices, tickets, and tasks from any Sharp app in conversation |
| External Communication | Collaborate with partner organizations and customers — even without a Sharp account |
| Search & AI            | Message search, conversation archives, AI summaries, and smart search         |

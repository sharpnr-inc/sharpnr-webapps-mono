Folio organizes content into workspaces and connects it to the rest of the Sharp platform.

## Shareable workspaces & permissions

Content lives in workspaces that teams share. Permissions control who can view, comment, and edit — scoped to workspaces or individual items, and consistent with the platform's role-based access model.

## Tight integration across the Sharp platform

Folio content connects to the rest of your work:

- Share documents, sheets, and boards directly in [Cables](/cables) conversations.
- Manage proposals and documents alongside deals in [Sharp-Marketing](/sharp-marketing).
- Keep HR documents and policies connected to [Sharp-HR](/sharp-hrms) workflows.

Underneath, content is stored on [Silo](/silo), the platform's data foundation.

## API-first architecture

Like the rest of the Sharp platform, Folio is API-first — its capabilities are available programmatically for integrations and automations, with credentials managed through [Cortex](/cortex/keys-and-secrets).

Winning a customer is half the job — Sharp-Marketing also handles keeping them.

## Loyalty & rewards programs

Run loyalty and membership programs that reward repeat business. Operation apps plug straight in — [Sharp-Hospitality](/hospitality-sector/sharp-hospitality) runs its customer loyalty and membership programs through Sharp-Marketing.

## Referral program management

Turn happy customers into acquisition: manage referral programs and track the customers they bring.

## Feedback, surveys & polls

Ask customers what they think — feedback management, surveys, and polls — and track responses where the customer relationship already lives.

## Customer support integration

Support interactions connect into the customer record, so sales, marketing, and support all see the same relationship.

## Engagement tracking & notifications

Track how customers engage across touchpoints, and manage the notifications that keep them informed without overwhelming them.

## Customer portal

Give customers a portal of their own — a place to interact with your organization directly.

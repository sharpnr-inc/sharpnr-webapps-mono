Sharp-Accounting turns your books into the statements, insights, and controls your organization runs on.

## Financial statements

The standard statements are built in:

| Statement                | What it shows                                        |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Balance Sheet            | Assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time    |
| Profit & Loss Statement  | Income and expenses over a period                     |
| Cash Flow Statement      | Cash movements over a period                          |
| Trial Balance            | All ledger balances, for verification and closing     |

Statements are available per legal entity, or [consolidated across the organization](/sharp-accounting/multi-entity).

## General ledger & custom reports

Drill into the books with general ledger reports, and build custom financial reports when the standard ones don't answer the question.

## Financial dashboards & analytics

Dashboards give finance teams and stakeholders a live view of financial performance — powered by books that update in real time from [connected operations](/sharp-accounting/platform-integration).

## Tax calculation & filing

Calculate taxes on transactions and support the filing process, keeping tax handling inside the same system as the books it's based on.

## Financial compliance & audit trails

Every entry carries its history — who recorded it, when, and from where (a user, Smart Entry, or an automatic posting from a business app). Audit trails support financial compliance and give auditors a complete, traceable record.

## Role-based approval workflows

Sensitive financial actions can require approval before taking effect. Approval workflows are role-based, so organizations control who can initiate, who must approve, and at what thresholds.

Sharp-CRM automates how organizations reach customers — across every channel customers actually use.

## Multi-channel campaigns

Run campaigns where your audience is:

- **Email marketing** — from one-off sends to automated sequences.
- **SMS & WhatsApp campaigns** — direct channels with high reach.
- **Social media campaign management** — plan and manage social campaigns alongside the rest.

Campaign management ties it together, with performance measured in [analytics](/sharpcrm/analytics-and-integrations).

## Customer segmentation

Segment customers by behavior, attributes, and history, so campaigns speak to the right audience rather than everyone at once.

## Form builder, lead capture & scoring

Build forms that capture leads directly into the [CRM](/sharpcrm/crm), with lead scoring highlighting the prospects most worth pursuing.

## Customer journey automation

Automate the path from first touch to conversion: journeys respond to what customers do — a signup, a visit, a purchase — with the right next message, without manual campaign babysitting.

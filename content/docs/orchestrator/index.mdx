Orchestrator is the central management layer of Sharpnr Suite, responsible for managing apps, organizations, and their configurations.

## What is Orchestrator?

Orchestrator acts as the control plane for the entire Sharpnr Suite. It handles:

- **App Management** — register, configure, and monitor all apps in the suite
- **Organization Management** — manage organizations and their settings
- **App Orchestration** — control how apps communicate and interact

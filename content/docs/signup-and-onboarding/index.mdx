Sharpnr is a cloud-based SaaS ecosystem — there is nothing to install or self-host. Getting onboarded means creating an organization account and switching on the apps your business needs.

<Callout title="Early Access">
  Sharpnr is currently in Alpha. The platform is free during the early access period; pricing will be announced later, and you will pay only for the applications and services you use.
</Callout>

## Create your organization

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Sign up

    Go to [auth.sharpnr.com/signup](https://auth.sharpnr.com/signup) and create an organization account. Your organization is your tenant on the platform — companies, branches, users, and apps all live under it.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign in

    Existing organizations sign in at [auth.sharpnr.com](https://auth.sharpnr.com). One account gives access to every Sharp app your organization has enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Open Orchestrator

    Head to [Orchestrator](/orchestrator), the platform command center at [orchestrator.sharpnr.com](https://orchestrator.sharpnr.com). This is where your organization takes shape — every Sharp application is provisioned and managed from here.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set up your structure and apps

    Define your [companies, branches, and legal entities](/orchestrator/organization-management), then [enable the apps](/orchestrator/app-management) each company needs — an operations app like [Sharp-Hospitality](/hospitality-sector/sharp-hospitality), core services like [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting) and [Cables](/cables), or all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Bring in your team

    Grant administrative access through [Orchestrator](/orchestrator/access-and-security), and give employees their day-to-day portal with [SharpIAM](/sharp-iam).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get from day one

- **Modular by design** — enable only the apps you need; add more anytime as your business grows.
- **Multi-everything** — multiple companies, branches, industries, and legal entities under one organization.
- **A verified B2B identity** — your organization is automatically eligible for [Sharp-Marketplace](/sharp-marketplace), with one-click onboarding and no separate registration.

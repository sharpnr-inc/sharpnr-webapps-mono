Cables is not a chat app bolted onto the platform — it is the communication layer every Sharp service connects to, with deep context integration across all Sharp applications and workflows.

## Share work, not screenshots

Records, documents, invoices, tickets, and tasks from other Sharp apps can be shared directly within conversations:

- Discuss an invoice from [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting) in the finance channel.
- Share a document from [Folio](/folio) for review in a project chat.
- Route a task or ticket to the person who can act on it.

The conversation and the work stay connected, instead of context living in one system and discussion in another.

## Communication inside workflows

Because Cables is part of the platform, workflows in other apps can reach people through it — notifications, approvals, and updates arrive where teams already communicate. Cross-app workflows are orchestrated through [Orchestrator](/orchestrator/configuration).

## One layer for the whole ecosystem

Every Sharp app an organization enables gains the same communication layer — no per-app chat silos, and no external conferencing product to integrate. Enable Cables once through [Orchestrator](/orchestrator/app-management) and it connects across the ecosystem.

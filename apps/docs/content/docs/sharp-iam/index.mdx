## What is SharpIAM?

SharpIAM is the self-service employee portal of the Sharp platform. It is where employees and managers access company resources, manage teams, complete daily tasks, and stay connected with the organization — from anywhere.

While [Sharp-HR](/sharp-hrms) is where the HR team runs the organization, SharpIAM is where everyone else experiences it: your payslips, your leave, your tasks, your team.

**App:** [iam.sharpnr.com](https://iam.sharpnr.com)

## What can you do with SharpIAM?

| Capability            | Description                                                             |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Employee Dashboard    | One secure login to everything work-related                              |
| Self-Service          | Leave, expenses, payslips, timesheets, documents, and profile            |
| Tasks & Schedule      | Assigned tasks, shifts, and the holiday calendar                         |
| Team & Directory      | Team management, org directory, and manager approvals                    |
| Stay Informed         | Announcements, notifications, reminders, and activity history            |

## Connected with the entire platform

SharpIAM is the employee's window into the whole Sharp ecosystem — HR data from [Sharp-HR](/sharp-hrms), communication through [Cables](/cables), documents from [Folio](/folio) — behind one secure employee login.

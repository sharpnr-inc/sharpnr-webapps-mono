Communication doesn't stop at your organization's edge — Cables extends securely to partners, vendors, and customers.

## Cross-organization collaboration

Collaborate with external partners and vendors in shared conversations. For organizations that trade through [Sharp-Marketplace](/sharp-marketplace), Cables is the communication channel for deals — quotations sent from [Sharp-Marketing](/sharp-marketing) can arrive directly in a Cables conversation with the buyer.

## Customer communication — no account required

Customers don't need a Sharp account to talk to you. Cables supports customer communication directly, so support and sales conversations happen on the platform without forcing customers to sign up for anything.

## Temporary guest links

For meetings and conversations with external participants, temporary guest links grant time-limited access — a customer can join a video call or a conversation through a link, which expires when it's no longer needed.

## Secure at the boundary

External communication follows the same enterprise-grade security as internal messaging: encryption, role-based access control, and administrative oversight of what crosses the organization boundary.

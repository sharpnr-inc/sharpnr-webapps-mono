## What is Folio?

Folio is the documents and productivity suite of the Sharp platform. Create, collaborate, and organize your knowledge in one place — documents, spreadsheets, presentations, notes, whiteboards, and PDFs, all with seamless real-time collaboration.

Because Folio is native to the platform, your organization's documents live where the rest of your work lives — shareable in [Cables](/cables) conversations, governed by platform permissions, and stored on [Silo](/silo).

**App:** [folio.sharpnr.com](https://folio.sharpnr.com)

## What can you do with Folio?

| Capability            | Description                                                        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Editors               | Documents, spreadsheets, presentations, Markdown, and PDF annotation |
| Notes & Knowledge     | Notes and a knowledge base for organizational knowledge            |
| Whiteboards           | Visual thinking and diagramming, together in real time             |
| Collaboration         | Real-time co-editing, comments, and version history                |
| Workspaces & Sharing  | Shareable workspaces with permissions, integrated across the platform |

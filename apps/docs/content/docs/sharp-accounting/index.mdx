## What is Sharp-Accounting?

Sharp-Accounting is the finance and accounting application of the Sharp platform. It is built on **double accrual accounting principles out of the box**, with support for additional accounting methodologies coming soon, and it works regardless of industry or sector.

Use it either way:

- **Standalone** — a complete accounting solution on its own.
- **Connected** — linked with Business Operation Apps like [Sharp-Hospitality](/hospitality-sector/sharp-hospitality), where every sale and purchase automatically posts journal entries — no manual ledger recording.

**App:** [accounting.sharpnr.com](https://accounting.sharpnr.com)

## What can you do with Sharp-Accounting?

| Capability               | Description                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Core Accounting          | General ledger, AP/AR, Smart Entry, bank reconciliation, recurring transactions  |
| Automatic Integration    | Journal entries posted automatically from business apps; purchase-to-inventory accounting |
| Multi-Entity Finance     | Multiple companies and legal entities, consolidated statements, multi-currency   |
| Financial Management     | Cash flow, budgeting, fixed assets and depreciation, expenses and reimbursements |
| Reporting & Compliance   | Financial statements, dashboards, tax calculation and filing, audit trails, approval workflows |

## Why Sharp-Accounting?

- **No manual re-entry** — when connected to operation apps, transactions flow into the books automatically as they happen.
- **Any structure, one platform** — separate books per legal entity or consolidated reporting across the organization, with parent, child, and subsidiary relationships.
- **Any industry** — the same accounting engine serves hotels, restaurants, clinics, retail, and mixed businesses alike.
- **API-first** — integrates with the rest of the Sharp platform and beyond.

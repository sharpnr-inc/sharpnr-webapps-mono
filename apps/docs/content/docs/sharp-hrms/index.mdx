## What is Sharp-HR?

Sharp-HR is a comprehensive Human Resource Management System (HRMS) for managing employees, payroll, attendance, recruitment, and workforce operations from a single platform. It supports multi-company and multi-branch organizations, with role-based access control throughout.

Employees interact with HR through [SharpIAM](/sharp-iam), the self-service employee portal — Sharp-HR is where the HR team runs the organization behind it.

**App:** [hr.sharpnr.com](https://hr.sharpnr.com)

## What can you do with Sharp-HR?

| Capability                  | Description                                                              |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Employee Management         | Employee records, recruitment, onboarding/offboarding, org chart, documents |
| Payroll & Attendance        | Payroll, time tracking, leave and holidays, shifts and rosters             |
| Performance & Development   | Reviews, goals and OKRs, training and learning                             |
| Employee Experience         | Self-service, expense claims, grievances, announcements, benefits          |
| HR Analytics                | Workforce reports and analytics across companies and branches              |

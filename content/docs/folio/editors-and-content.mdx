Folio brings every content type an organization works with into one suite — no separate office products, note apps, and whiteboard tools to stitch together.

## Documents

Rich text documents for everything from meeting notes to formal policies, with rich media embedding for images, video, and content from across the platform.

## Spreadsheets

Spreadsheets for calculations, lists, and lightweight data work — collaborative like everything else in Folio.

## Presentations

Build and deliver slide decks without leaving the platform.

## PDF viewing & annotation

View PDFs and annotate them directly — contracts, reports, and scans get reviewed where they're stored, not downloaded and emailed around.

## Whiteboards

Visual collaboration for brainstorming, diagrams, and planning sessions — sketch together in real time.

## Markdown editor

A dedicated Markdown editor for those who prefer writing in plain text with structure.

## Notes & knowledge base

Capture notes and build them into an organizational knowledge base, so what the team knows stays findable long after the conversation that produced it.

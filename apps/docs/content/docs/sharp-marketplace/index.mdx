## What is Sharp-Marketplace?

Sharp-Marketplace is a B2B marketplace exclusively for organizations on the Sharp platform. Businesses buy and sell products and services with each other through a trusted, verified network — **with no commission and no charges**.

It is the Sharp platform's fourth layer: organization-to-organization (O2O) connections. A furniture supplier on the platform can serve hotels, hospitals, and offices; a produce wholesaler can supply the restaurants — every organization is both a business running its operations and a potential trading partner.

**App:** [marketplace.sharpnr.com](https://marketplace.sharpnr.com)

## What can you do with Sharp-Marketplace?

| Capability             | Description                                                              |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Buying & Selling       | Trade goods and services with verified organizations, cross-industry       |
| RFQs & Discovery       | Request quotations, compare vendors, and discover suppliers with AI        |
| Procurement            | Purchase orders and invoices created directly from marketplace transactions |
| Trust & Verification   | Automatic organization verification and secure business identity           |

## Why trade on Sharp-Marketplace?

- **No commission, no charges** — the marketplace doesn't take a cut of your trade.
- **Verified counterparties only** — every participant is a verified organization on the Sharp platform.
- **Deals connect to your systems** — quotations arrive through [Cables](/cables), purchase orders and invoices land in [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting), and procurement flows into your operation apps.

Sharp-CRM measures what marketing and sales actually produce — and connects to the systems around them.

## Conversion, ROI, and attribution

- **Conversion tracking** — which leads become customers, and where they drop off.
- **ROI analytics** — what campaigns cost against what they return.
- **Attribution tracking** — which touchpoints deserve credit for a conversion.

## Dashboards & KPIs

Dashboards keep pipeline, campaign performance, and engagement KPIs in view for the people who need them — from marketers to stakeholders viewing the organization through [Orchestrator](/orchestrator).

## Third-party integrations

Connect Sharp-CRM with external tools and services. Like the rest of the platform it is API-first, with credentials managed through [Cortex](/cortex/keys-and-secrets).

## Native platform connections

The deepest integrations come built in:

- Quotations flow to [Cables](/cables) or the recipient's preferred channel — including [Sharp-Marketplace](/sharp-marketplace) counterparties.
- Deals connect to invoicing in [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting).
- Loyalty programs power [Sharp-Hospitality](/hospitality-sector/sharp-hospitality) memberships.

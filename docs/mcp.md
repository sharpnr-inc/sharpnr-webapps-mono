Overview

Sharpnr is a modular orchestration platform designed for real-world hospitality operations such as hotels, restaurants, cafés, bars, and multi-outlet brands.

It brings together operations, kitchens, accounting, and intelligence into a single, extensible system — without forcing businesses into bloated or rigid workflows.

Sharpnr is free to start and scales only when you do.

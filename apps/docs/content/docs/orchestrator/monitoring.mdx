Orchestrator is the observation deck for your Sharpnr ecosystem. Administrators see the state of every enabled service from one dashboard, without logging into each app.

## Service health & status

The administration dashboard shows the live status of every Sharp service enabled for your organization — what is running, what is degraded, and what is stopped. From the same view, administrators can [start, stop, or restart services](/orchestrator/app-management#service-control).

## Logs & diagnostics

View logs and diagnostics for platform services directly in Orchestrator. When something behaves unexpectedly, administrators can inspect what happened without leaving the command center.

## Resource usage

Monitor how much of the platform's resources each service and company consumes. Resource usage visibility helps organizations understand their footprint — and, once pricing is announced, what they are paying for.

## Alerts & notifications

Platform alerts notify administrators when a service needs attention — health changes, failures, or administrative events. Notifications keep the operations team responsive without requiring them to watch the dashboard.

## Maintenance mode

Maintenance mode controls let administrators take services offline in a controlled, communicated way — for example during planned configuration changes — and bring them back when ready.

## Audit trail

Every administrative action in Orchestrator is recorded. See [Access & Security](/orchestrator/access-and-security) for audit logs and administrative activity tracking.

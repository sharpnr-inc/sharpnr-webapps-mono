Sharp-Marketplace makes trading between organizations as direct as the rest of the platform — publish what you offer, find what you need, and negotiate in one place.

## Selling: publish without catalog management

Publishing products and services is hassle-free — no catalog management required. Any organization on the platform can offer what it sells to the network, whether goods, services, or both.

## Buying: discovery across industries

Discover new business partners and procurement opportunities across a cross-industry network — furniture suppliers serving hotels, hospitals, and offices; food wholesalers serving restaurant groups; service providers serving everyone.

**AI-powered supplier discovery** recommends suppliers based on what your organization needs, so finding the right vendor doesn't depend on already knowing they exist.

## Request for Quotation (RFQ) & vendor comparison

Send RFQs to suppliers and compare vendors side by side before committing. Suppliers respond with quotations — sent from their [Sharp-Marketing](/sharp-marketing) CRM directly to your [Cables](/cables) or preferred communication channel.

## Deals and contracts

Finalize deals and contracts with your counterparty on the platform, then let [procurement](/sharp-marketplace/procurement) carry the transaction into your operational systems.

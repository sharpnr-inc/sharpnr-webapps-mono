Silo holds the organization's data, so it is built to not lose it, not leak it, and not run out of room.

## Backup & replication

Data in Silo is backed up and replicated, protecting the organization against data loss and keeping data available.

## Encryption & access control

Data is encrypted, and access is controlled: who can read, write, or administer any database or dataset is governed through [Cortex](/cortex/access-control), with Read, Write, and Admin levels scoped per resource. Every access is subject to the platform's audit logging.

## Scalable distributed storage

Silo is a scalable, distributed storage platform — it grows with the organization, from a single small business to a multi-company enterprise, without migration projects along the way.

Sharp Accounting is the finance and accounting module of Sharpnr Suite, providing tools for managing finances, invoices, and reports.

## What is Sharp Accounting?

Sharp Accounting provides:

- **Invoice Management** — create and manage invoices
- **Financial Reports** — generate detailed financial reports
- **Expense Tracking** — track and categorize expenses

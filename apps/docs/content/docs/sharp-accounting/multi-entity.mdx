Sharp-Accounting is built for organizations that are more than one company — multiple legal entities, mixed industries, and operations across currencies.

## Multi-company & multi-legal entity support

Each company in your organization can be a separate legal entity with its own independent accounting books and financial records. A group operating a hotel company, a restaurant company, and a clinic keeps clean, legally distinct books for each — all within one platform.

The organization structure itself — companies, branches, and legal entities — is defined in [Orchestrator](/orchestrator/organization-management), and Sharp-Accounting builds its books on that structure.

## Parent, child, and subsidiary relationships

Model your corporate structure as it really is: parent companies, children, and subsidiaries, with ownership relationships reflected in the books.

## Separate books or consolidated statements

Choose the financial structure that matches how your business reports:

- **Separate books per entity** — each legal entity closes and reports independently.
- **Consolidated financial statements** — entity books roll up through parent-child relationships into consolidated statements for the whole organization.

You don't have to pick one forever — entities keep their own books either way; consolidation is a reporting view on top.

## Multi-currency support

Operate and record transactions in multiple currencies — essential for organizations whose entities trade across borders or serve international customers.

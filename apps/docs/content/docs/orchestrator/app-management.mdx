Every application in the Sharpnr ecosystem is provisioned and managed through Orchestrator. This is where you decide which apps each company uses and control the services behind them.

## Enable or disable applications

Sharpnr is modular — organizations pay for and use only the applications they need. From Orchestrator you can:

- **Enable an app for a company** — the app is provisioned and becomes available to that company's users.
- **Disable an app** — the app is switched off for that company without affecting other companies in the organization.
- **Mix apps per company** — each company can run a different combination, since every app works both independently and as part of the unified platform.

## Service control

Beyond enabling apps, Orchestrator gives administrators direct control over the services that power them:

- **Start, stop, and restart** Sharp services.
- **Maintenance mode** — take a service offline for maintenance in a controlled way.
- **Service health and status** — see at a glance what is running and what needs attention (see [Monitoring](/orchestrator/monitoring)).

## Apps work independently — and better together

Enabling apps through Orchestrator is what unlocks Sharpnr's out-of-the-box integration. For example:

- Business operation apps can run completely independently, without requiring other Sharp platform services.
- When [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting) is enabled alongside [Sharp-Hospitality](/hospitality-sector/sharp-hospitality), sales and purchases automatically post journal entries — no manual ledger recording.
- When [Cables](/cables) is enabled, records, documents, invoices, tickets, and tasks from other apps can be shared directly within conversations.

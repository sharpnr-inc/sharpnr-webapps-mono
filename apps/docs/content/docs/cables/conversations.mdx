Cables organizes communication the way organizations actually talk — from private messages to company-wide announcements.

## Chats and channels

- **One-to-one chats** — direct, private conversations between two people.
- **Group chats** — conversations for teams, projects, or any ad-hoc group.
- **Organization-wide channels** — channels every member can follow, for announcements and shared topics.

## Threaded conversations

Threads keep busy channels readable: replies attach to the message they answer instead of scrolling the whole channel. Conversations support rich media and file sharing, so screenshots, documents, and files live where they're discussed.

## Search, history, and archives

Everything said in Cables stays findable — full message search, conversation history, and archives. AI-powered conversation summaries and smart search help catch up on long conversations without reading every message.

## Secure by default

Communication in Cables is fast, encrypted, and low-latency. Access to conversations follows role-based access control, backed by the platform's enterprise-grade security.

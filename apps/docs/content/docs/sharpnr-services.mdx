## Business Operation Apps

Where the [platform services](/sharpnr-platform) are horizontal — accounting, HR, storage, messaging — the business operation apps are vertical. Each one models the day-to-day workflow of a specific industry, and each sits on the same shared foundation: one identity, one organization, one set of financial records.

That means a restaurant bill raised in Sharp-Hospitality and a patient invoice raised in Sharp-Healthcare both land in [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting) as ordinary journal entries. You do not reconcile between systems, because there is only one system.

| App | Industry | What it handles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Sharp-Hospitality](/hospitality-sector/sharp-hospitality) | Hotels, restaurants, bars, nightclubs | Reservations, guest services, POS, table and room management, multi-property operations |
| Sharp-Healthcare | Clinics and hospitals | Patient records, appointments, clinical workflows, billing and claims |

## How they fit together

Every business operation app is provisioned through [Orchestrator](/orchestrator) and inherits the organization structure defined there — companies, branches, and the users attached to them. Access is governed by [Cortex](/cortex), so a receptionist at one property does not silently gain access to another.

The shared services underneath are the same for every vertical:

- **[Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting)** — charges, invoices, and payments from any app become accounting entries
- **[Sharp-HR](/sharp-hrms)** — staff, rosters, and payroll across every location
- **[Silo](/silo)** — documents and records storage
- **[Cables](/cables)** — internal messaging between teams and locations

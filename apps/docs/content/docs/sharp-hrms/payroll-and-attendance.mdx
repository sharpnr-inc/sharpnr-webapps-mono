Sharp-HR runs the operational heartbeat of the workforce: who worked, when, and what they're paid.

## Payroll management

Run payroll across the organization. Because Sharp-HR shares a platform with [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting), payroll connects to the organization's books without manual re-entry, and employees access their payslips through [SharpIAM](/sharp-iam).

## Attendance & time tracking

Track attendance and working time. Timesheets are visible to employees in the portal, and attendance data feeds payroll directly.

## Leave & holiday management

Manage leave policies, applications, balances, and the holiday calendar. Employees apply for leave and track approvals in SharpIAM; managers approve from the same portal.

## Shift & roster scheduling

Plan shifts and rosters — essential for operations like hotels and restaurants that run around the clock. [Sharp-Hospitality](/hospitality-sector/sharp-hospitality) staff scheduling works hand in hand with Sharp-HR's workforce management.

Sharpnr is a cloud-based SaaS platform — there is nothing to install or self-host. Getting started with Orchestrator means creating an organization account and signing in.

<Callout title="Early Access">
  Sharpnr is currently in Alpha. The platform is free during the early access period; pricing will be announced later.
</Callout>

## 1. Create your organization

Sign up at [auth.sharpnr.com/signup](https://auth.sharpnr.com/signup) to create an organization account. If your organization already exists, sign in at [auth.sharpnr.com](https://auth.sharpnr.com) instead.

For a full walkthrough of account creation, see [Signup & Onboarding](/signup-and-onboarding).

## 2. Open Orchestrator

Once signed in, open [orchestrator.sharpnr.com](https://orchestrator.sharpnr.com). Orchestrator is the first app every organization uses — it is where the rest of the ecosystem gets switched on.

## 3. Set up your organization structure

Before enabling apps, define how your business is structured:

- **Companies** — add each company that operates under your organization.
- **Branches** — add branches or properties under each company.
- **Legal entities** — if you run multiple legal entities, decide whether each keeps separate accounting books or reports into consolidated financial statements.

See [Organization Management](/orchestrator/organization-management) for details.

## 4. Enable the apps you need

Sharpnr is modular — organizations use only the applications they need. From Orchestrator, enable apps per company:

- An industry operations app such as [Sharp-Hospitality](/hospitality-sector/sharp-hospitality) for hotels, restaurants, bars, and nightclubs.
- Core services such as [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting), [Sharp-HR](/sharp-hrms), [Sharp-Marketing](/sharp-marketing), [Cables](/cables), [Silo](/silo), [Folio](/folio), and [Cortex](/cortex).

See [App Management](/orchestrator/app-management) for the full list and how enabling works.

## 5. Invite your team

Grant administrative access with role-based permissions, and let employees access their day-to-day tools through [SharpIAM](/sharp-iam), the employee portal. See [Access & Security](/orchestrator/access-and-security).

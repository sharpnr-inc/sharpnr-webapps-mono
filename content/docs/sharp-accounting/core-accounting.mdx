Sharp-Accounting's core is a complete double accrual accounting engine — everything an accounting team needs to keep accurate books.

## General ledger management

The general ledger is the heart of Sharp-Accounting. Every transaction — entered manually, posted by Smart Entry, or recorded automatically from a connected business app — lands in the ledger under double accrual accounting principles.

## Accounts payable & receivable

Manage both sides of your organization's obligations:

- **Accounts Payable (AP)** — track what you owe suppliers, manage bills, and schedule payments.
- **Accounts Receivable (AR)** — track what customers owe you, manage invoices, and follow up on collections.

## Smart Entry

Smart Entry speeds up manual recording for invoices, receipts, and journal entries — capturing documents into properly structured accounting entries with far less typing than traditional data entry.

## Bank reconciliation

Match your books against bank activity to catch discrepancies early and keep ledger balances trustworthy.

## Recurring transactions & scheduled entries

Rent, subscriptions, standing charges — define them once and Sharp-Accounting records them on schedule, so predictable transactions never depend on someone remembering to enter them.

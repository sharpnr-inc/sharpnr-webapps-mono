Sharp-HR covers not just employing people, but growing them.

## Performance reviews & appraisals

Run structured review cycles — appraisals, feedback, and outcomes recorded against the employee record, visible to employees and managers through [SharpIAM](/sharp-iam).

## Goals & OKR tracking

Set goals and OKRs at the individual and team level and track progress through the cycle, keeping reviews grounded in agreed objectives rather than recollection.

## Training & learning management

Manage training programs and learning across the workforce — what's required, who has completed what, and where certifications stand, connected to the [compliance documents](/sharp-hrms/employee-management) HR already tracks.

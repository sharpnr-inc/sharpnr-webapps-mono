Cables covers real-time communication as fully as messaging — voice, video, and the collaboration tools meetings need.

## Voice and video

Make voice calls and host video conferences directly in Cables — no separate conferencing product to buy or integrate. Calls carry the same encryption and low-latency performance as messaging.

## Screen sharing and meeting collaboration

Share your screen and use meeting collaboration tools to work through problems together — reviewing a document, walking through a report, or presenting to the team.

## Presence and notifications

Real-time presence indicators show who is available, and real-time notifications make sure messages, mentions, and calls reach people promptly — without them living inside the app all day.

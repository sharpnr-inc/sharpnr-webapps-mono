Cortex is growing from access management into a complete IT management layer for the Sharp platform. The following capabilities are coming soon.

<Callout title="Coming Soon">
  The features on this page are planned and not yet available. Sharpnr is in Alpha, and the platform evolves continuously — new capabilities ship regularly.
</Callout>

## Device Management

Enroll, configure, and manage the devices your organization uses — bringing company hardware under the same central administration as users and resources.

## Endpoint Security

Protect the endpoints that access the Sharp platform, extending Cortex's security controls from the cloud down to the devices themselves.

## Asset Inventory

Track IT assets across the organization — what exists, where it is, and who is responsible for it — alongside the access controls that govern them.

## Policy Enforcement & Approval Workflows

Move from defining policies to enforcing them automatically, with approval workflows that require sign-off before sensitive access is granted.

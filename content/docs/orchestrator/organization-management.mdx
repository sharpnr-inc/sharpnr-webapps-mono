Orchestrator is where you model your business structure. Every Sharp application reads this structure, so companies, branches, and legal entities are defined once and used everywhere.

## The organization hierarchy

Sharpnr is multi-tenant, multi-company, and multi-branch by design:

| Level        | What it represents                                                                  |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Organization | Your tenant on the Sharpnr platform — the top-level account                         |
| Company      | A business unit or legal entity within the organization                             |
| Branch       | A physical or operational location under a company — a hotel property, a clinic, a store |

A single organization can manage multiple industries and business sectors at once. A mixed business — for example a group that operates hotels, restaurants, retail stores, and a hospital — runs everything from one platform, with each company enabling only the apps it needs.

## Legal entities and accounting books

Each company in your organization can be a separate legal entity with its own independent accounting books and financial records. Orchestrator lets you choose the financial structure that matches how your business is organized:

- **Separate books per entity** — each legal entity maintains its own general ledger, statements, and compliance records in [Sharp-Accounting](/sharp-accounting).
- **Consolidated reporting** — parent, child, and subsidiary relationships roll up into consolidated financial statements across the organization.

## Multi-branch operations

Branches let a company operate multiple locations under shared configuration. Business operation apps use this directly — for example, [Sharp-Hospitality](/hospitality-sector/sharp-hospitality) supports multi-property management, shared kitchens across multiple businesses, and shared menus with items routed to different kitchens.

## Stakeholder visibility

Beyond administration, Orchestrator gives stakeholders a view of the whole organization — every company, branch, and enabled service — without requiring them to work inside each individual app.

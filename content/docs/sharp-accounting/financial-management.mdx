---

Beyond bookkeeping, Sharp-Accounting gives finance teams the tools to plan, control, and manage the organization's money.

## Cash flow management

Track cash moving in and out of the organization so finance teams always know the current position and can anticipate what's ahead — fed by real-time books rather than month-old snapshots.

## Budgeting & financial planning

Set budgets and plan financially across the organization. Because actuals flow in automatically from [connected business apps](/sharp-accounting/platform-integration), comparing plan against reality doesn't wait for a closing cycle.

## Fixed asset & depreciation management

Register fixed assets and manage their depreciation over time — asset values and depreciation entries stay current in the ledger without manual period-end calculations.

## Expense & reimbursement management

Manage organizational expenses and employee reimbursements. Employees can submit expense claims through [SharpIAM](/sharp-iam), the employee portal, and [Sharp-HR](/sharp-hrms) handles the workforce side of claims — with the financial records landing in Sharp-Accounting.
